Open SpeakEz

First time? Three steps, about two minutes.

  1. Install Expo Go. It is the free app that runs SpeakEz.
    Expo Go for iPhone→ Expo Go for Android→
  2. Open Expo Go once and sign in. Any free Expo account works, or Continue with Google — it is the fastest. Expo Go may refuse to open a project until an account exists on the device. Nothing is shared with SpeakEz: the account belongs to Expo, and SpeakEz never sees it.
  3. Come back here and tap Open SpeakEz. Expo Go takes over from there.

Stuck on the sign-in? Ask us for one of the phones on the table — they are already signed in and ready.

Already have Expo Go? Scan this from inside the app.

exp://__GO_HOST__:8081

First load takes 20–60 seconds while the bundle builds. After that it is quick.
